How the measurement works

The tool averages pixel luminance across a horizontal band. The selected outermost threshold crossing defines an image boundary. A least-squares line fits boundary position against time. Repeating the extraction at threshold ±10 shows sensitivity to one processing choice; it is not a confidence interval. Optical motion is not automatically the material's flame-spread rate. Exclude igniters, camera movement, overlays, and background lights; verify calibration and image timestamps.

Method inspiration: BASS-II report §2.2.4, printed p. 22 ↗

    The wattage is not the whole story.

    NASA’s BASS-II report describes a prolonged opposed thick-slab burn near 100 W that damaged a window corner, while a briefer concurrent-rod burn near 110 W did not. The contrast suggests duration, geometry, and heat path matter alongside heat-release rate. Two examples do not establish a universal threshold.
